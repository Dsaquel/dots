GLASSHOUSE ART PACK
===================
The art, films, sounds and fonts the Glasshouse theme reads from ~/.local/share/glasshouse.
Installed by glasshouse-pack install; every file is listed with its sha256 in SHA256SUMS
(check with: glasshouse-pack verify, or sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS inside the pack).

What runs code
--------------
Only ledger/scene: a Godot 4 project (scripts/*.gd, shaders/*.gdshader) the settings
panel (Super+I) starts with godot --path. Everything else is data: png, jpg, mp4, mkv,
wav, json, ttf.
